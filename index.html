📜 ESTATE GUIDE & CONTROLS

Navigation: W A S D to walk, Mouse Drag to orbit camera, Scroll to zoom.
The Three Zones:
1. Front Chaya (Tea House): Serve arriving wanderers and brew bowls at the iron hearth kettle.
2. Samurai House (Omoya): Located directly behind the tea house through the wooden gate. Your private living quarters with sword display rack and tatami rooms.
3. Backyard (Behind the House): Tilled soil plots to sow seeds [2, 3], water [1], and harvest tea leaves [4], plus an open courtyard to decorate [B].
Zen Kata: Press F to draw your katana in a graceful meditation kata for +5 Zen.
